## Needs a human
- This edition's narrator mostly calls Fagin "the Jew"; the characters call him Fagin.
  His facts are filed under `fagin`. Should the narrator's epithet be listed as an alias
  in the roster? Dickens cut most uses of it in 1867 (see `sources.md`).
- The walk is travel data (distance, days, daily distances), but this base doesn't enable
  the travel module, so it's recorded as values in `places.md` and on Oliver's sheet.
  Should the base enable travel, with routes for the high road and the way in from
  Islington?
- The glossary has its own table (`Term`, `Meaning`, `Kind`), which the record's columns
  don't match. These rows put the term's id in `Entity`, its kind in `Attribute`, and its
  meaning in `Value`. Is that the intended mapping?
- The journey is in winter, but with no calendar the season can only be a value.
- The Dodger is first named in scene 3, as "Jack Dawkins", and the narrator then calls
  him "John Dawkins". Both are recorded as aliases of one slug.

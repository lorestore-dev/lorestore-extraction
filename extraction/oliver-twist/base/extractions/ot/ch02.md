## Needs a human
- The world has no calendar, so the chapter's spans (eight or ten months by hand, nine
  years at the farm, six months of the new system, three months of hunger) can only be
  recorded as values. A calendar counted from Oliver's birth would let them be dated:
  should the worldbuilder define one?
- Scene 1 is a summary covering nine years. The farm is dated at OT:2:2, where Oliver is
  first shown there, with `True from` OT:2:1. Is that the right use of `True from` for a
  summary, rather than for revealed backstory?
- The chairman is "the gentleman in the high chair" until scene 6 names him. His facts
  are filed under `mr-limbkins` from scene 3; this isn't a reveal alias, since nothing is
  hidden.
- The workhouse board is an institution, not a character. It's filed in `society.md` as
  `workhouse-board`; confirm the file.

## Needs a human
- Oliver was nine on his ninth birthday (OT:2:2) and says he's ten here. Time has passed
  between the two, so it isn't a contradiction, but nothing records how much: with no
  calendar, the record can't tell.
- The Baytons are named only by the surname on the coffin order. The slugs `mr-bayton`
  and `mrs-bayton` assume the dead woman and her husband share it.
- "the porochial surgeon" here is filed under `parish-surgeon`, the slug proposed for
  the surgeon at Oliver's birth. Nothing in this unit says they're the same man.

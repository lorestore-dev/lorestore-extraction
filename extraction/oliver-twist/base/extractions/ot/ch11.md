## Needs a human
- Mr. Brownlow is "the old gentleman" robbed at the bookstall. If an earlier unit
  proposed a slug for him unnamed, this name should merge with it.
- The unit gives a weekday (Monday morning) and speaks of "Her Majesty's subjects". With
  no calendar, neither can be recorded as a date. Is "Her Majesty" the narrator's own
  time, not the story's?
- "Tom White" is a false name given in court, not an alias Oliver uses. It's left out
  of the roster and recorded only in the `standing` value.
- Mr. Brownlow's sense that Oliver's face is familiar reads as foreshadowing. It's
  recorded as his impression only, never as a fact about Oliver's family.
